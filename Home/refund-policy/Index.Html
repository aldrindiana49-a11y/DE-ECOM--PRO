Refund Policy

Drin Electronics values customer satisfaction and aims to provide quality products and services.

Eligibility for Refund

Refund requests may be accepted for defective, damaged, incorrect, or non-functioning items subject to verification and review.

Refund Conditions

Items must be returned in their original condition, including packaging, accessories, and proof of purchase whenever applicable.

Non-Refundable Items

Certain products such as customized items, improperly used products, or items damaged by misuse may not qualify for refunds.

Refund Processing

Approved refunds may take several business days depending on payment providers, banking processes, and order verification.

Shipping Costs

Shipping fees may not be refundable unless the issue was caused by an incorrect shipment or verified seller error.
